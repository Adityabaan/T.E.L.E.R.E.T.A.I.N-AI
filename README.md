📖 𝐎𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰

Customer churn is one of the most expensive, underestimated problems in the telecom industry. Acquiring a new customer costs 𝟓–𝟕𝐱 𝐦𝐨𝐫𝐞 than retaining an existing one — yet most retention strategies are reactive, kicking in only after a customer has already left.

𝐓.𝐄.𝐋.𝐄.𝐑.𝐄.𝐓.𝐀.𝐈.𝐍. 𝐀𝐈 flips that model. It's an end-to-end machine learning pipeline that:

🔍 Predicts 𝐰𝐡𝐢𝐜𝐡 customers are likely to churn, before they churn
💡 Explains 𝐰𝐡𝐲 they're likely to churn, using real behavioral and hardware signals
💰 Simulates the 𝐚𝐜𝐭𝐮𝐚𝐥 𝐟𝐢𝐧𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐢𝐚𝐥 𝐑𝐎𝐈 of acting on those predictions

The result: a proof of concept showing that even a modestly-accurate model (~63%) can generate 𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐥, 𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐯𝐞 𝐑𝐎𝐈 when paired with smart, targeted business action.

──────────────────────────────

❓ 𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐛𝐥𝐞𝐦

📉 Highly saturated, price-sensitive telecom market (India-focused, generalizable globally)
💸 Rising Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), shrinking margins
🗃️ Companies already have rich usage & billing data — but rarely act on it proactively
🎯 𝐆𝐨𝐚𝐥: Shift from reactive, blanket marketing to a precise, AI-driven early-warning retention system

──────────────────────────────

🗂️ 𝐃𝐚𝐭𝐚𝐬𝐞𝐭

🧹 After cleaning → 𝟗𝟖 𝐟𝐢𝐧𝐚𝐥 𝐟𝐞𝐚𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞𝐬
⚖️ Near-perfectly balanced classes: 𝟓𝟎,𝟒𝟑𝟖 𝐫𝐞𝐭𝐚𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐝 vs 𝟒𝟗,𝟓𝟔𝟐 𝐜𝐡𝐮𝐫𝐧𝐞𝐝 — a realistic, non-trivial classification problem

🔬 𝐄𝐱𝐩𝐥𝐨𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐲 𝐃𝐚𝐭𝐚 𝐀𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐲𝐬𝐢𝐬 — 𝐓𝐰𝐨 𝐁𝐮𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐬𝐬 𝐇𝐲𝐩𝐨𝐭𝐡𝐞𝐬𝐞𝐬

1️⃣ 𝐇𝐚𝐫𝐝𝐰𝐚𝐫𝐞 𝐅𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐠𝐮𝐞 📱
Customers with older handsets are more likely to churn — aging devices make them susceptible to competitor upgrade offers.

2️⃣ 𝐒𝐢𝐥𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐃𝐞𝐩𝐚𝐫𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 📉
A gradual decline in Minutes of Use (MoU) is a leading indicator of churn — customers quietly disengage weeks before formally cancelling.

✅ Both hypotheses were validated visually (box plots) 𝐚𝐧𝐝 independently confirmed later by model feature importance rankings.

🤖 𝐌𝐨𝐝𝐞𝐥𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐑𝐞𝐬𝐮𝐥𝐭𝐬

𝐑𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐨𝐦 𝐅𝐨𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐭 (300 trees): 62.76% accuracy
𝐗𝐆𝐁𝐨𝐨𝐬𝐭 ⭐ (lr=0.03, depth=8): 𝟔𝟐.𝟖𝟐% 𝐚𝐜𝐜𝐮𝐫𝐚𝐜𝐲, 𝐑𝐎𝐂-𝐀𝐔𝐂 𝟎.𝟔𝟖𝟐, 𝟔𝟔% 𝐜𝐡𝐮𝐫𝐧 𝐫𝐞𝐜𝐚𝐥𝐥, 𝟔𝟐% 𝐜𝐡𝐮𝐫𝐧 𝐩𝐫𝐞𝐜𝐢𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧

🎚️ Optimal decision threshold (scanned 0.10–0.90): 𝟎.𝟓𝟎 — the default cutoff was already near-optimal

🧠 𝐓𝐡𝐞 "𝐀𝐜𝐜𝐮𝐫𝐚𝐜𝐲 𝐏𝐚𝐫𝐚𝐝𝐨𝐱": a naive always-predict-retained model would also score ~50% but generate 𝐳𝐞𝐫𝐨 𝐛𝐮𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐬𝐬 𝐯𝐚𝐥𝐮𝐞. This model deliberately trades a little raw accuracy for a much higher churn-catch rate — because catching churners, not maximizing accuracy, is what protects revenue.

🔑 𝐓𝐨𝐩 𝐂𝐡𝐮𝐫𝐧 𝐃𝐫𝐢𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐬 (𝐅𝐞𝐚𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐈𝐦𝐩𝐨𝐫𝐭𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞)
1. eqpdays — equipment age (confirms Hardware Fatigue)
2. months — tenure
3. hnd_webcap — handset web capability
4. refurb_new — refurbished/new device flag
5. mou_Mean — average minutes of use (confirms Silent Departure)

💸 Total campaign cost: $60,000
🛡️ Gross revenue protected: $77,300
📈 𝐍𝐞𝐭 𝐑𝐎𝐈: +$𝟏𝟕,𝟑𝟎𝟎

𝐓𝐰𝐨 𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐞𝐝 𝐫𝐞𝐭𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧 𝐥𝐞𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐬:

💡 𝐊𝐞𝐲 𝐢𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭: Targeted precision beats blanket spend — a model with only ~63% raw accuracy still generated real, positive ROI because it ranks risk well, not because it's perfectly accurate.
